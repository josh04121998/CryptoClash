Privacy.

What Floorwars stores, what it never stores, and how to get rid of it. Written to be read, not to be survived.

← Back to the game Litepaper Terms

The short version

You can play Floorwars without giving us anything. No account, no wallet, no email. If you connect a wallet, we store your public wallet address and the game progress attached to it — nothing else about you.

We never ask for, receive, or store: your name, email address, phone number, postal address, date of birth, government ID, payment card details, your wallet's private key, or your seed phrase. Nobody will ever ask you for a seed phrase on this site. If something claiming to be Floorwars does, it isn't us.

What gets stored, and when

1. Before you do anything

Nothing is stored on our servers when you land on the site. You can read the litepaper, play the tutorial, and play against the AI without anything of yours reaching us. Those modes run entirely in your browser.

2. In your own browser

Floorwars uses no cookies at all. It stores a few values in your browser's local storage, which stay on your device and are cleared whenever you clear site data:

KeyWhat it is
cryptoclash.anonIdA random identifier created by your browser. It is not derived from anything about you, and it identifies a browser, not a person.
cryptoclash.sessionYour sign-in token, if you connected a wallet. It is what keeps you signed in.
cryptoclash.mutedWhether you muted the sound.
cryptoclash.tutorialCompleted
cryptoclash.tutorialSkipped		So we stop offering you the tutorial.
cryptoclash.referralCodeSet only if you arrived through someone's invite link, so their reward can be credited.

3. If you connect a wallet

Connecting uses Sign-In With Ethereum: your wallet signs a message to prove you control the address. That signature proves control and nothing more — it cannot move funds, and we never see your private key.

We then store, against your wallet address:

  • your public wallet address, lowercased, and when the account was created;
  • your card collection, saved decks, Coins, Dust, and packs;
  • your quests, daily and weekly rewards, achievements, and ranked progress;
  • your referral code and whether an invite has been credited;
  • records of the online matches you play — the result, how it ended, how long it ran, and which factions were used.

A wallet address is a pseudonymous identifier: it does not name you, but a public blockchain may let it be linked to other activity. That is inherent to using a wallet anywhere, not specific to Floorwars — worth knowing before you connect one.

4. Product analytics

To know whether the game is any good, we record a small, fixed list of named events — things like "the app opened", "a match started", "a pack was opened". Each event carries the random browser identifier above, and your account id if you are signed in.

The list of event names is fixed in the code and enforced on our server: anything not on the list is discarded. The extra detail attached to an event only ever comes from a fixed set of values we chose in advance — a faction name, a match result, a number of turns. Nothing you type ever goes into analytics, including your deck names.

Analytics events never contain your IP address, your wallet address, your browser's user-agent string, the page you arrived from, or any free text. There is no advertising network, no cross-site tracking, and no third-party tracking pixel anywhere in the game.

5. Your IP address

Our server sees your IP address, as every web server does. It is used in memory only, to stop one source hammering sign-in or analytics, and it is never written to our database. Our hosting providers keep their own connection logs (see below).

We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with advertisers. There is nothing to sell.

How long it is kept

  • Browser storage — until you clear your site data. You control this entirely.
  • Account and game progress — until you ask us to delete it.
  • Analytics events and match records — kept as long as they are useful for improving the game. If your account is deleted, these are detached from it rather than destroyed: they keep the game's aggregate statistics honest, but stop pointing at you.

Getting rid of it

For anything in your browser: clear site data for this site, and it is gone. We cannot see it and cannot recover it.

For your account: ask, and we will delete the account and the progress attached to it. Because a wallet address is the only identifier we hold, you will need to prove you control that wallet — the same signature you use to sign in.

Depending on where you live you may also have the right to request a copy of what we hold, to have it corrected, or to object to analytics. Ask and we will do it; we would rather not hold data you do not want us to have.

Children

Floorwars is not designed or intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect anything from them.

Changes

If this changes in a way that affects what we collect, the version and date at the top of this page will change and the difference will be described here rather than quietly slipped in.
