What this is

Floorwars is a trading-card game you play in a browser. By using it you accept what is on this page. If you do not, that is fine — do not use it.

Floorwars is in active development. Things will change, break, and occasionally be reset. Card stats, rewards, odds and prices are all being balanced and will move. Treat anything you have in the game as temporary until we say otherwise.

It is a game, not a financial product

Coins, Dust, cards, packs and ranks exist inside the game. They have no cash value, we do not offer to buy them back, and they are not an investment, a security, or a stake in anything.

Nothing in the game, the litepaper, or anything we post is financial or investment advice. We are not licensed to give any, and we are not giving any. If you make a financial decision because of something you read here, that decision is yours alone.

Wallets

You never need a wallet to play. If you connect one, you are connecting it yourself, through software we do not control, and you remain responsible for it.

We will never ask for your seed phrase or private key, and we never need them. Anyone who asks you for them — anywhere, claiming to be us — is trying to steal from you.

Signing in asks your wallet to sign a message proving you control the address. It does not move funds and does not grant us spending permission.

Playing fairly

Do not:

  • automate play, script the client, or run bots to farm rewards;
  • abuse the referral system with accounts you control;
  • exploit a bug for gain instead of telling us about it;
  • attack the service, or try to reach other players' data;
  • use the game where doing so breaks your local law.

If you do, we may remove progress obtained that way or close the account. We would much rather hear about a bug than find out you farmed it — tell us and we will say thank you.

Your account

Your account is tied to your wallet address. If you lose access to that wallet, we cannot recover the account — there is no password to reset and no other identifier we hold. That is the trade-off of wallet sign-in, and it is worth understanding before you rely on it.

You can ask us to delete your account at any time. See Privacy.

What we promise, and what we do not

We promise to run the game as well as we can and to be straight with you about what it is.

We do not promise it will be available, uninterrupted, bug-free, or preserved forever. It is provided as it is. To the fullest extent the law allows, we are not liable for losses arising from using it — and given that nothing in it has cash value, the honest expectation is that the worst case is lost time and a lost card collection.

Content

The game, its cards, art, and text are ours. Enjoy them, screenshot them, stream them, post them. Do not pass them off as your own or use them to make something that pretends to be Floorwars.

These terms will change as the game grows, particularly if on-chain ownership arrives. The version and date at the top change when they do, and material changes will be described rather than quietly slipped in.
