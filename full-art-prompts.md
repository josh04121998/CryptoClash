# Floorwars — Full Art Prompts (Founders Set)

### v1.0 — 2026-09-29

Ready-to-paste Grok prompts for the Full Art Edition illustrations `collectibility.md` Section 13 item 5 and `branding.md` §9.3 flagged as real, not-yet-started generation work. Same slow, background, no-rush grind as `grok-card-prompts.md` — go one card at a time.

## Scope — why these 11 and not all 77

The Founders Set can technically reprint any card at Full Art (`collectibility.md` §8 — "a Founders Set can reprint an existing Common in Full Art just as easily as a Legendary"), but generating 77 speculative Full Art prompts before the Founders Set has even sold once would be pure waste. **This first batch is the 11 existing Legendary-rarity creatures** — the roster's natural flagships, already the most narratively significant card per faction, and the real-TCG-standard place to start (Pokémon's first Full Art sets curated around their flagship EX/GX Pokémon the same way). Nothing stops adding more cards to a later Founders Set once there's real signal for what players actually chase.

The 11: Loyal Hound & Alpha Dog (Doggos), Deep Croak & Primordial Croak (Frogs), Full Stack Grizzly & Unicorn Ursa (Bears), Moon Ape & Exit Silverback (Apes), Compound Brahman & Unicorn Bull (Bulls), Steadfast Tabby (Cats — the faction's only Legendary).

## One illustration per card, not three — Ultra and Secret are not separate art

`collectibility.md` §4 describes Full Art, Ultra and Secret as three Edition tiers, which reads like three things to generate. It isn't. Re-reading the tier descriptions closely: **Full Art is the new illustration itself** ("alternate, full-bleed illustrated artwork"); **Ultra is "premium presentation layered on top of Full Art"** (animated/foil-treatment-heavy); **Secret is "the top chase tier,"** always serialized and hard-capped, but nothing in its own description says it needs different art from Full Art. That's the exact same "no new art asset" pattern this whole document already uses for Foil and Condition — both are pure CSS/compositing layers over one underlying illustration, never a second generated image. Applying it here: **this file only needs one new prompt per card.** Ultra and Secret become a border/foil-treatment/serial-styling distinction at render time (a future `render-card.mjs`/`CardFace.tsx` change — not built yet, out of scope for this file, which is art-generation only).

## What actually makes Full Art different from the Standard prompt

Not a new character, not a new concept — the *same* creature, attire, colors and scene elements as the card's existing Standard illustration (listed under each prompt below for reference), re-composed wider per `branding.md` §9.3: **the illustration now fills the entire card canvas edge-to-edge, top to bottom** (the compositor's real 1500×2100 / 2:3 canvas — see `tools/card-render/render-card.mjs`), not the smaller art-window sub-rectangle Standard art sits in. Concretely: more of the character's body is shown, more environment and depth, real vertical scale — the opposite instruction from Standard's own "medium shot, close to camera" rule. That rule existed to survive being shrunk to a 52×76px board slot (`grok-card-prompts.md`'s v7 "silhouette survives shrinking" lesson) — Full Art never renders at board-slot size, so it doesn't apply.

One shared technical requirement across all 11, appended to each concept below rather than repeated in full each time:

> Full-bleed composition filling the entire portrait card canvas edge to edge, top to bottom, exactly 2:3 aspect ratio — not the compact art window Standard-edition prints use. Semi-realistic illustrated character art, anime-adjacent rendering with real lighting and shading — not flat vector, not photorealistic, not a literal wildlife photograph. [Faction accent color] rim light and background glow, with a warm neutral key light on the subject itself so it separates cleanly from the backdrop — an elaborate, premium Full Art composition with real depth and scale, clearly more expansive than the character's own Standard-edition portrait. The scene should read as one continuous illustration that gradually simplifies and darkens toward the bottom third (same trading-floor palette, not a hard cutoff), so a name/stats text overlay stays legible there later without needing a separate blank panel. No text, no numbers, no logos, no watermark, no signature, no card border or frame, no rarity glow baked in, no readable words anywhere in the scene.

## Once these exist — the code side, not yet built

Not part of this file's scope (art generation only), noted here so it isn't lost: once a Full Art illustration exists for a card, `tools/card-render/render-card.mjs` needs an actual `editionType` branch (today it accepts the field but always renders Standard art regardless — see `STATUS.md` session 37) to pick the right source image, and `client/src/cardArt.ts`/`CardFace.tsx` would need their own decision about whether Full Art ever renders in the live game board (today nothing does — `branding.md` §9.3: "the client doesn't even have a slot to render this yet"), or stays an NFT/collection-screen-only presentation. That's a real product question, not decided here.
